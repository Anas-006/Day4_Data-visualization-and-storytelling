Data Visualization and Storytelling – Superstore Sales

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating meaningful visualizations from the Sample Superstore dataset and presenting business insights through a clear visual story.

Objective

To analyze sales performance and communicate trends, category performance, and regional patterns using data visualization.

Tools Used

- Tableau
- Sample Superstore Dataset

Visual Story

Page 1 – Business Overview

Provides an overall view of sales, profit, and order performance.

Takeaway: The overview shows the overall sales, profit, and order performance of the Superstore business.

Page 2 – Sales Trend

Shows how sales performance changes over time.

Takeaway: Monthly sales trends help identify periods of stronger and weaker business performance.

Page 3 – Category Performance

Compares sales performance across product categories.

Takeaway: Category-wise sales comparison helps identify the strongest-performing product categories.

Page 4 – Regional Performance

Compares sales performance across different regions.

Takeaway: Regional sales comparison highlights differences in business contribution across regions.

Page 5 – Key Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes the major findings and provides business recommendations.

Key Insights:

- Sales performance varies across different months.
- Product categories contribute differently to total sales.
- Regional sales performance differs across regions.

Recommendations:

- Focus on high-performing categories and regions.
- Investigate weaker sales periods to understand possible causes.
- Use monthly and regional trends for better sales planning.

The project demonstrates how multiple visualizations can be organized into a simple business story to help non-technical stakeholders understand sales performance and make data-driven decisions.
